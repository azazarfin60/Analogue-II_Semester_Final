## 📊 Exam Strategy Reminder
> Fully solving these 5 items statistically covers 70-80% of the exam:
> 1. Cascaded JFET-BJT amplifier (memorize the exact circuit)
> 2. 555 timer astable (charging and discharging plus design)
> 3. Wien bridge oscillator (pick R and C for target f₀)
> 4. Op-amp summing, integrator, and differentiator design
> 5. CMRR and slew rate definitions and calculations
